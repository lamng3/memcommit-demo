Playground

Write memories into an append-only log, say what each was derived from, retract a premise, and watch what was built on it go stale. Drag the log back in time, or roll it back.

Warning

This playground runs a JavaScript port of MemCommit's core, not the library. MemCommit is Python. The port covers content-addressed memories, restore on rewrite, retraction and invalidation with their cascades, rollback by compensating records, and as_of, and its tests replay scenarios recorded from the Python library and compare every id and log record. It does not port query references, guards, tiers beyond the first, or sessions, notes, and dreaming.
